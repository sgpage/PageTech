DetectLogPro

The ultimate tech tool for Detectorists

DetectLogPro is an advanced tool for Metal Detectorists giving you a place to record and manage your finds, and with the Premium upgrade, plan trips using historic maps and Lidar, print reports and labels, and sync everything to Google Drive.

📲 Download Free

Powerful Map Interface

The map screen is the heart of DetectLogPro. Browse finds as thumbnails right where you found them, toggle Lidar overlays, track your route, and filter by date, type or hashtag. Works beautifully on phones, tablets and desktop.

Lidar & Historic Map Overlays

Import MBTiles files generated from DEFRA survey data or other sources and overlay them on any custom map. Reveal hidden field boundaries, old pathways and earthworks invisible to the naked eye. Add up to six custom NLS historic OS maps for unrivalled trip planning.

Organise Your Finds

Search, sort and manage every discovery. Long-press to select multiple finds for batch reporting. The smart search recognises both text and find ID. Deleted finds can be recovered until your next sync.

Photo Gallery

Browse every photo across all your finds in one beautiful gallery. Tap a picture to jump straight to the full record. Images lazy-load to keep things snappy, even with thousands of finds.

Reports & 2D Label Printing

View hashtag and PAS counts, generate standard reports, and print professional 3×7 labels complete with scannable QR codes that link straight back to the find record in the app. Fully configurable for different printers and label stock.

Google Drive Cloud Sync

Connect to your Google Drive to back up your entire database. Share the DetectLogProSync folder with detecting partners — their finds appear on your map with a blue surround. Export or import everything in one tap.

⌚

Apple Watch App

Leave your phone in your pocket — add finds using voice dictation straight from your Apple Watch, even with muddy gloves! The watch also mirrors your current map view, including Lidar overlays, so you always know where you are.
