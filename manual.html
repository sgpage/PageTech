Introduction

Welcome to DetectLogPro, the comprehensive mobile application designed specifically for metal detecting enthusiasts. DetectLogPro helps you document, organize, and analyze your metal detecting finds with powerful mapping, photography, and reporting features.

This manual will guide you through all the features of DetectLogPro, from basic find logging to advanced mapping overlays and cloud synchronization. Whether you're a hobbyist or a serious detectorist, DetectLogPro provides the tools you need to make the most of your detecting adventures.

💡 Getting Started Tip

We recommend starting with the Maps section to familiarize yourself with the interface, then move on to Adding Finds to log your first discovery!

Maps

The Maps interface is the heart of DetectLogPro, providing you with powerful visualization tools to track your detecting locations and finds. The app offers both desktop and mobile views optimized for different use cases.

Adding Finds

Recording your finds is quick and intuitive with DetectLogPro. The app automatically captures your GPS location and allows you to add detailed information about each discovery.

Figure 4: Add Find screen with all available fields

Find Entry Fields

When adding a find, you can record:

  • Date & Time: Automatically captured, but can be edited
  • Location: GPS coordinates automatically recorded
  • Find Type: Select from categories (coin, artifact, jewelry, etc.)
  • Description: Detailed notes about your find
  • Depth: How deep the item was buried
  • Material: Silver, gold, copper, iron, etc.
  • Condition: Rate the condition of your find
  • Estimated Period: Historical period (Roman, Medieval, Victorian, etc.)
  • Photos: Attach multiple photos of the find
  • Notes: Additional observations or context

⚠️ Important

Always ensure your GPS is enabled before adding finds. While you can manually enter coordinates later, automatic GPS capture ensures the most accurate location data.

Quick Add Mode

For rapid logging in the field, enable Quick Add mode which captures only essential information (location, time, and a photo). You can fill in additional details later.

URL Maps (NLS MapTiler)

DetectLogPro supports custom map tile servers, allowing you to overlay historical maps from the National Library of Scotland (NLS) and other sources.

Figure 5: Configure custom map tile URLs for historical overlays

Adding Custom Map Sources

To add a custom map tile server:

  1. Navigate to Settings > Map Sources
  2. Tap "Add Custom Map"
  3. Enter a name for your map source
  4. Paste the tile URL (e.g., NLS MapTiler URL)
  5. Set the minimum and maximum zoom levels
  6. Save and activate your new map layer

💡 NLS Maps

The National Library of Scotland provides excellent historical OS maps. Visit their website to get tile URLs for maps from different time periods. These are invaluable for identifying old settlements and potential detecting sites.

Supported Tile Formats

DetectLogPro supports standard XYZ tile formats. The URL should include {z}, {x}, and {y} placeholders:

https://example.com/tiles/{z}/{x}/{y}.png

List Features

Pictures Tab

The Pictures tab provides a gallery view of all your find photographs, making it easy to review and share your discoveries.

Figure 7: Picture gallery with thumbnail grid view

Photo Management

  • Gallery View: Browse all photos in a grid layout
  • Full Screen: Tap any photo for full-screen viewing
  • Photo Editing: Crop, rotate, and adjust brightness
  • Annotations: Add arrows and labels to highlight features

Photography Tips

📸 Best Practices

  • Photograph finds against a neutral background
  • Include a scale reference (ruler or coin) in the image
  • Take photos in natural daylight when possible
  • Capture multiple angles of interesting finds
  • Clean finds gently before photographing for better detail

Overlays

Map overlays allow you to layer additional information on top of your base map, including historical maps, LIDAR data, and custom boundaries.

Figure 8: Multiple overlay layers showing historical maps and boundaries

Overlay Types

DetectLogPro supports several overlay types:

  • Historical Maps: Victorian OS maps, old surveys, and historical charts
  • LIDAR Data: Elevation data revealing hidden earthworks
  • Cadastral Maps: Property boundaries and field divisions
  • Custom Overlays: Import your own georeferenced images
  • Find Density: Heat maps showing concentration of finds

Managing Overlays

  1. Tap the Overlays icon in the ribbon menu
  2. Toggle overlays on/off with the eye icon
  3. Adjust transparency using the opacity slider
  4. Reorder layers by dragging them
  5. Add new overlays from file or URL

💡 Historical Research

Overlaying modern satellite imagery with Victorian OS maps can reveal lost settlements, old pathways, and former field boundaries - all excellent indicators of potential detecting sites!

Reports

DetectLogPro includes powerful reporting features to help you analyze your finds, create documentation, and generate labels for your collection.

Figure 9: Reports interface with multiple report types available

Report Types

  • Find Summary: Statistical overview of all finds by type, period, and material
  • Location Report: Detailed report for a specific detecting location
  • Date Range Report: Finds within a specified time period
  • Photo Album: Printable photo collection with descriptions
  • GPS Export: Export coordinates in GPX or KML format

Generating Reports

  1. Open the Reports section
  2. Select your desired report type
  3. Configure report parameters (date range, filters, etc.)
  4. Preview the report
  5. Export as PDF, print, or share via email

2D Label Printing

The 2D Label Printing feature generates professional labels for your finds collection, complete with QR codes for quick database lookup.

Figure 10: 2D label printing interface showing barcode and QR code options

Label Options

  • Label Size: Choose from standard sizes (30mm x 15mm, 40mm x 20mm, etc.)
  • QR Code: Includes unique identifier linking to find record
  • Text Fields: Select which information to display (date, type, location)
  • Font Size: Adjustable for different label sizes
  • Batch Printing: Print labels for multiple finds at once

📌 Label Printer Compatibility

2D labels are compatible with most thermal and standard printers. For best results with small labels, use a dedicated label printer like Brother QL series or DYMO LabelWriter.

Import from GPKG

DetectLogPro can import GeoPackage (GPKG) files, allowing you to bring in data from GIS applications, other detecting apps, or shared databases.

Figure 11: Import interface for GeoPackage files

Import Process

  1. Navigate to Settings > Import/Export
  2. Select "Import from GPKG"
  3. Choose your GPKG file from device storage or cloud
  4. Map GPKG fields to DetectLogPro fields
  5. Preview the import data
  6. Confirm import

Field Mapping

During import, you'll need to map the GPKG columns to DetectLogPro fields:

  • Required: Longitude, Latitude (or geometry field)
  • Optional: Date, Type, Description, Photos
  • Custom Fields: Additional columns can be imported as custom attributes

⚠️ Data Validation

Always preview your import before confirming. Check that coordinates are correct and dates are properly formatted. Invalid data will be flagged for review.

Supported Geometry Types

  • Point (individual finds)
  • LineString (search paths or boundaries)
  • Polygon (search areas or fields)

Cloud Sync

Keep your finds synchronized across all your devices with DetectLogPro's cloud sync feature. Your data is securely backed up and available wherever you need it.

Setting Up Cloud Sync

  1. Open Settings > Cloud Sync
  2. Create a PageTech account or sign in
  3. Choose what to sync (finds, photos, settings)
  4. Select sync frequency (manual, daily, or real-time)
  5. Enable sync and wait for initial upload

Sync Options

  • Automatic Sync: Data syncs whenever changes are made (requires internet)
  • Manual Sync: Sync only when you tap the sync button
  • WiFi Only: Avoid mobile data usage by syncing only on WiFi
  • Selective Sync: Choose specific locations or date ranges to sync

💡 Battery & Data Conservation

To save battery and mobile data while detecting, enable "WiFi Only" sync. Your finds will be stored locally and uploaded automatically when you return to WiFi coverage.

Privacy & Security

Your data is encrypted during transmission and storage. You maintain full control over your data:

  • End-to-end encryption for sensitive location data
  • Export all data at any time
  • Delete cloud data completely if desired
  • Share specific finds or locations without exposing your entire database

⚠️ Account Security

Use a strong, unique password for your PageTech account. Enable two-factor authentication for additional security, especially if you're documenting valuable finds.

Conflict Resolution

If the same find is edited on multiple devices, DetectLogPro will:

  • Automatically merge non-conflicting changes
  • Alert you to conflicts requiring manual resolution
  • Show both versions side-by-side for comparison
  • Allow you to keep one version or merge them manually

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How accurate is the GPS location tracking?
DetectLogPro uses your device's GPS which is typically accurate to within 5-10 meters in open areas. For more precise location marking, allow the app a few moments to acquire a strong GPS signal before recording a find. Accuracy can be reduced in wooded areas or near tall buildings.
Q2: Can I use DetectLogPro offline?
Yes! DetectLogPro works fully offline. Map tiles are cached automatically as you use them, and all finds are stored locally on your device. You can sync your data to the cloud when you have internet access. We recommend downloading the map areas you plan to detect before heading out.
Q3: How much storage space do photos use?
Photos are automatically optimized for storage while maintaining quality. A typical find photo uses 200-500KB. You can adjust photo quality in Settings > Camera Options. The app will alert you when storage is running low and offer to sync older photos to the cloud to free up space.
Q4: Can I export my data to use in other applications?
Yes, DetectLogPro supports multiple export formats including CSV (for spreadsheets), GPX (for GPS applications), KML (for Google Earth), and GPKG (for GIS software). Go to Settings > Import/Export to access all export options.
Q5: Is my find location data private?
Absolutely. Your location data is stored securely and is never shared without your explicit permission. When using cloud sync, data is encrypted both in transit and at rest. You can also use the app entirely offline if you prefer not to use cloud features.
Q6: Can multiple users share a database?
Yes, you can create shared workspaces that allow multiple detectorists to collaborate. This is perfect for clubs or detecting partners. Each user can add finds, and all data syncs between authorized devices. Manage sharing settings in Settings > Workspace Sharing.
Q7: How do I report significant finds to the authorities?
For UK users, DetectLogPro includes a PAS (Portable Antiquities Scheme) report generator. Select your find and choose "Generate PAS Report" from the Reports menu. This creates a properly formatted report with all required information. For treasure finds, always contact your local Finds Liaison Officer promptly.
Q8: What happens if I lose my device?
If you have cloud sync enabled, all your data is safely backed up. Simply install DetectLogPro on your new device, sign in with your PageTech account, and all your finds will sync automatically. This is another great reason to enable cloud backup!
Q9: Can I customize the find categories and fields?
Yes, DetectLogPro allows you to create custom find types and add custom fields to suit your specific needs. Go to Settings > Custom Fields to add new categories, materials, or any other attributes you want to track. Your custom fields will appear in all relevant screens and reports.
Q10: Does DetectLogPro work on both Android and iOS?
Yes, DetectLogPro is available for both iOS (iPhone and iPad) and Android devices. Your data syncs seamlessly between platforms if you use cloud sync, so you can use an Android phone in the field and review your finds on an iPad at home.
