Privacy Policy for ScalePlate

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Introduction

ScalePlate ("we," "our," or "the app") builds scaled photographic plates of your finds from your own photos. This Privacy Policy explains what the app does with your information.

In short: ScalePlate does not collect, transmit or store your data on any server. Everything you create stays on your device unless you choose to save or share it.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in, no passwords
  • No data is sent to PageTech or to any third party
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting
  • No location data is accessed
  • The app works fully offline
  • Photos and plates are only saved or shared when you choose

What the App Uses

The app only uses information you give it, and only to do the job you ask of it.

1. Photos

What: Photos you take with the in-app camera, choose from your photo library, or pick from a file.

Why: To calibrate, crop, cut out and combine into a finds plate.

Where it goes: Photos are processed on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere. Working copies are kept temporarily on your device while you build a plate.

2. Finished Plates

A plate is only saved to a file or to your photo library when you tap Save file or Save to Photos. It is only sent elsewhere when you choose to share it, or use Add to DetectLogPro (see below).

3. Find ID, Date and Measurements

The Find ID, date and calibration distances you enter are used to label and scale the plate. They stay on your device.

4. Settings

Your preferences, such as plate options, are stored locally on your device so the app remembers them. They are never transmitted.

5. Camera, Motion Sensors and Barcode Scanning

  • Camera: to photograph finds against a scale, and to scan a DetectLogPro label code when attaching a plate to a find. Camera images are processed on your device and are not transmitted.
  • Motion sensors: to show whether your phone is level when taking a photo. Sensor readings are used live on your device and are not stored or transmitted.

What the App Does NOT Collect

  • Your name, email address or any account details
  • Your location (GPS or otherwise)
  • Your contacts, calendar or any other personal data
  • Usage analytics, device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Crash reports sent to us or to anyone else

Integration with DetectLogPro

If you have our DetectLogPro app installed, Add to DetectLogPro passes a finished plate to it on your device so it can be attached to a find record. This happens between the two apps on your device and does not go through any server. How DetectLogPro handles your data is described in its own privacy policy. If DetectLogPro is not installed, the app offers to open its store page.

Permissions Explained

Camera

  • When: Taking photos of finds and scanning find label codes
  • Control: Optional. You can deny it and choose photos from your library or files instead

Photo Library

  • When: Choosing existing photos, and saving finished plates when you ask
  • Control: Optional. You can deny it and use files instead

Motion Sensors (iOS)

  • When: Showing whether your phone is level while taking a photo
  • Control: Optional

Links to Other Websites

The app contains links, for example to our website and support pages. These open in your device's browser or app store and are outside the app. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Data Storage, Security and Retention

  • Everything you create is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage or in the locations you choose to save to
  • Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot access, recover or delete it for you
  • Plates you have saved to your photo library, files or shared with others are outside the app. You manage them with your device
  • Uninstalling the app removes its working data and settings

Children's Privacy

ScalePlate is not directed at children under 13. Since the app does not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase. This applies equally to residents of the EEA, UK (GDPR) and California (CCPA). We do not sell or share personal information. If you have any concerns, please contact us below.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we change how the app works in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding a feature that sends data off your device, we will update this policy, change the "Last Updated" date at the top, and tell you in the app before it takes effect.
